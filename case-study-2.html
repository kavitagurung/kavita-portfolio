CASE FILE 03 / CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE

Predictive Retention Engine

Finding behavioural signals before high-value customers disengage.

Overview

A customer intelligence workflow designed around behavioural signals, segmentation, dashboards, and intervention opportunities.

The problem

Teams needed a way to recognise meaningful changes in customer behaviour before disengagement became visible in a simple outcome metric.

My role

Requirements, dashboard and workflow definition, recommendation touchpoints, and stakeholder alignment.

Key product decisions

01 / SIGNAL

Start with observable behaviour.

Context: Retention problems are hard to act on when they are detected only after the customer has disengaged.

Decision: Focus the workflow on behavioural signals and the patterns they reveal.

Why it mattered: Product and customer teams have an earlier starting point for investigation and action.

02 / SEGMENT

Make a large customer base legible.

Context: The same signal may mean different things for different customer groups.

Decision: Use segmentation and customer context alongside behavioural data.

Why it mattered: Teams can evaluate a pattern with more context than a single aggregated view provides.

03 / ACTION

Design for a next step.

Context: A dashboard has limited value if it does not support a decision or intervention workflow.

Decision: Define recommendation touchpoints alongside the analytics experience.

Why it mattered: Insight can move towards a customer or product action rather than remaining only a report.

Evidence and outcomes

  • Connected behavioural data, segmentation, dashboards, and recommendation touchpoints into one decision workflow.

What I learned

Prediction is only useful when the people receiving it understand the context, trust the signal, and can take a meaningful next step.
