CASE STUDY

Real-Time Analytics Workspace

A unified analytics experience designed to help teams monitor, investigate, and act on live product data.

Context

Teams needed faster access to live product signals without assembling reports across multiple tools.

My role

Translated stakeholder requirements into role-specific dashboard modules and led wireframing with UX partners.

Solution

Designed reusable dashboard views and drill-down flows to make monitoring and investigation more consistent.

Delivery

Used Figma, Whimsical, and Balsamiq to test and refine the information architecture before delivery.

Impact

Analytics dashboards saved users an estimated 5–10 hours each week by reducing reporting effort.

Confidentiality note

Production screens contain sensitive business data and are intentionally omitted from this public portfolio.
