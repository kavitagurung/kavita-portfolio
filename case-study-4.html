CASE STUDY

Roadmap Intelligence Program

A repeatable product-planning framework for connecting customer signals, market context, and delivery sequencing.

Context

Product decisions need a shared method for balancing market opportunities, customer urgency, effort, and delivery risk.

My role

Led roadmap planning, competitive analysis, and cross-functional alignment across product, engineering, and go-to-market teams.

Framework

Created a prioritization model that brought customer feedback, effort, impact, and urgency into the same decision conversation.

Operating model

Established collaboration rituals and milestone planning to make trade-offs visible and releases more predictable.

Success measures

Tracked roadmap confidence, delivery predictability, stakeholder alignment, and the follow-through on high-priority customer needs.

What this demonstrates

The program highlights product-operating skills: prioritization, communication, risk management, and delivery planning.
