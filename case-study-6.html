CASE STUDY

TestOrbit

An evidence-led research workspace for release and competitor intelligence in the test automation industry.

Challenge

Product and market teams need a reliable way to follow ecosystem changes without treating marketing language, inference, and verified product updates as the same thing.

Release tracking

Created a Release Watch that organizes product-release signals for Workday, ServiceNow, SAP SuccessFactors, and Coupa in one research workspace.

Competitor tracking

Configured tracking for 40 test-automation competitors and surfaces changes such as product releases, AI capabilities, integrations, and pricing or packaging updates.

Evidence model

Each insight links back to a public official publication and labels the evidence level, separating verified updates from positioning or marketing claims.

Decision support

The workspace brings recent signals, capability comparisons, and release coverage into a daily recap that can inform product discovery and roadmap conversations.
